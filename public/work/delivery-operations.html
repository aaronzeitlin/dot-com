Delivery operations and reporting

Microsoft · Contract · May 2018–February 2023

I joined Microsoft's Industry Solutions delivery organization in an operational role. Over time, the work moved from program coordination into technical program management and then primarily business intelligence and analytics.

What I worked on

  • Azure DevOps-based tracking and workflow improvements for global delivery programs.
  • Reporting and dashboards that connected operational activity with deal progress and cloud consumption measures.
  • Automation of repetitive reporting work and coordination across business and technical stakeholders.

How the role changed

The title on my profile describes the function, not a formal internal job level. As the team's needs became more technical, I increasingly owned the systems and analysis used to understand the work, rather than only coordinating its delivery.

What carried forward

That shift shaped how I work: understand the underlying business process first, then build the data and automation that make it visible and manageable.

This is a general account of internal contract work; customer and internal system details are omitted.
