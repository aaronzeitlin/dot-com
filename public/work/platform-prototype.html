Full-stack audio platform prototype

Independent engineering · Archived · Not launched

I built a prototype web platform for creators to upload, manage, and preview audio. It was an engineering project, not an operating business: it was archived before public launch and had no users.

What I built

  • A Next.js and React frontend with an Express API.
  • PostgreSQL with Prisma for users, audio uploads, and related records.
  • Authentication, role-based access, upload flows, activity features, and analytical views.
  • Object storage and presigned media URLs, with deployments across Vercel and Railway.

Engineering lessons

Working through media storage and playback made the differences between a working feature and a reliable end-to-end system very concrete. Authentication, permissions, browser behavior, storage URLs, and deployments have to agree across service boundaries.

Status

Archived. The prototype demonstrated a full-stack architecture but never reached a public launch or user adoption. I keep it as an example of practical systems-building, not as a product success story.
